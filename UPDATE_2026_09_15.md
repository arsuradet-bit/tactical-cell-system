# Workspace and camera timeline update

Verified on 15 September 2026:

- Workspace navigation preserves separate search, CDR, camera and area result sets. Camera points only appear in the camera workspace.
- Selecting a timeline row displays the associated camera and all G-Mon observations for that CDR event. Browser testing used synthetic records: three G-Mon points plus one camera.
- Camera summary counts matched camera records, camera records without nearby CDR, and camera records without coordinates separately.
- Excel contains matched timeline rows, follow-up records and matching conditions. Export also works without any matched timeline rows. Dates are Excel dates; text identifiers preserve leading zeros. Browser download was verified with synthetic files.
- Time-first camera values such as `18:57:44 19/07/2026` are accepted. Invalid camera rows are reported.
- Original G-Mon rows and append-only uploads are the search sources. The deduplicated migration table is no longer substituted for original records. No database data was changed.
- Read-only Cloud SQL checks found 42,914 legacy rows and 486 uploaded rows (43,400 total at verification time). An exact CELL/LAC sample returned all 94 legacy rows, matching an independent count. One sample query took approximately 1.6 seconds; this is not a concurrency or large-import benchmark.
- Search disables statement timeout within its own transaction, as requested. This does not prevent unrelated network or service interruptions.
- Existing 19 unit tests passed. Additional Streamlit AppTest checks verified workspace isolation and camera summaries. Excel round-trip checks verified matched and unmatched-only exports.

Camera matching remains a time-proximity comparison with VOICE priority per camera window. It does not establish that a phone or vehicle passed a checkpoint. CDR and camera uploads remain session-only.
